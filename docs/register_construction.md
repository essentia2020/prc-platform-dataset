# Register construction

The register started from a candidate enumeration drawn up in September 2026, which reproduced the platforms of Figure 1 in Corker, Waltman and Coates (2024) and was explicitly marked as a candidate list, not a mandatory inclusion list. Corker et al. supplied the conceptual definition of the PRC model and the original dataset that this register reconstructs and updates; it was not applied as a mechanical inclusion filter, and the enumeration's derivation beyond that figure is not documented in the project record.

Additions were searched in October 2026 by consulting the Sciety group directory and the F1000 publishing-venues page, and by targeted web searches against each candidate's own About, submission, FAQ and editorial-policy pages. Every inclusion or exclusion was recorded individually: included services in `config/platforms.yaml`, rejected and unverifiable candidates in `config/excluded_candidates.yaml` with `verification_status` and `failed_criterion`. The register also grew during extraction validation: seven further venues on the F1000 infrastructure were identified from unassigned Crossref DOI stems and added on 27 October 2026 (D-052), the last documented addition.

No formal stopping rule was applied; the search ended with the release freeze. The coverage claim of this release is therefore systematic with respect to verification (every candidate considered has a recorded disposition and evidence URL) but not with respect to search (no procedure guarantees that every eligible service was considered). An external review in November 2026 identified one omission, JMIRx Bio, which is handled in D-0xx. A search protocol with an explicit stopping criterion is planned for the next release.
