# Register construction

The register began with a candidate enumeration drawn up in September 2026.
It reproduced the platforms shown in Figure 1 of
Corker, Waltman and Coates (2024) and was marked as a candidate list, not a
list of mandatory inclusions. Corker et al. supplied the conceptual definition of the PRC model
and the original dataset that this register reconstructs and updates; their
list was not applied as a mechanical inclusion filter, and how the
enumeration was derived beyond that figure is not documented in the project
record.

Additions were sought in October 2026 from two directories, the Sciety group
list and the F1000 publishing-venues page, and through targeted searches of
each candidate's own About, submission, FAQ and editorial-policy pages. Every
inclusion and exclusion was recorded individually: included services in
`config/platforms.yaml`, rejected and unverifiable candidates in
`config/excluded_candidates.yaml` with `verification_status` and
`failed_criterion`. The register also grew during extraction validation:
seven further venues on the F1000 infrastructure were identified from
unassigned Crossref DOI stems and added on 27 October 2026 (D-052).

No formal stopping rule was applied; the search ended with the release
freeze. The coverage claim of this release is therefore systematic with
respect to verification, since every candidate considered has a recorded
disposition and an evidence URL, but not with respect to search, since no
procedure guarantees that every eligible service was considered. Two checks
in November 2026 illustrate the difference. An external review identified
one omission, JMIRx Bio, which enters the register under D-072. A comparison
with the PRC Alliance members list on 2026-11-05 found 18 member
organisations already represented, 2 in the excluded-candidates file and 23
without a match by organisation name; of the latter, eight services that may
operate an evaluation workflow were recorded as pending candidates under
D-072, and the remainder are funders, libraries and infrastructure bodies
rather than candidate observation series. A search protocol with an explicit
stopping criterion is planned for the next release.
